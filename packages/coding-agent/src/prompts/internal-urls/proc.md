`proc://`: jobs/services; `proc://<id>` status/output and service watchers. Write service stdin to `proc://<name>` (empty = Enter); `proc://<id>/kill` cancels/stops, omit `content`; service `proc://<name>/mode`: `persist`|`session`|`detached`.
Write `content: "wake"|"ambient"|"off"` to `proc://<id>/progress`. Services: attach/retune/detach this session's monitor; new attachment = future output only; off does not stop the service. Detached services cannot be live-monitored.
Jobs: retune caller-owned running jobs with an existing progress channel between wake/ambient only. Off is rejected; jobs launched without progress cannot gain a channel later. Queued output survives retuning; a pending wake may still arrive after switching to ambient.
Wake starts an idle follow-up turn; ambient rides active turns. Noisy? SHOULD lower source verbosity on safe relaunch, or use ambient (services also allow off). NEVER poll or call `wait` for wake progress; end the turn. Truncated/suppressed previews link the capture artifact.
